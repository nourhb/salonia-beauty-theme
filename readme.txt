== Description ==

Salonia is an elegant Full Site Editing theme crafted for beauty salons and spas — hair studios, nail bars, facial and massage therapists. Blush rose, deep rose and gold accents on warm ivory, with a booking-first hero, trust badges, services grid with pricing hints, stylist profiles, a pricing menu, testimonials, photo gallery, stats band, FAQ, hours & location section and a booking CTA — everything a salon needs to fill its chairs online.

A clean, modern Full Site Editing (block) theme built with theme.json. Includes multiple page templates, curated block patterns, and "Midnight" and "Blush" style variations. Translation-ready and accessibility-conscious.
